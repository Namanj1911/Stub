stub.

A cigarette-reduction tracker for iPhone and Android. Smoke less, on purpose.

Privacy policy

Everything stays on your phone. We collect nothing.

Stub has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Your log, goal, settings, and progress live only in the app's local storage on your device. The app sends none of your data anywhere — no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third parties. This is what Stub declares on both stores: Data Not Collected on the App Store, and No data collected and No data shared in the Data safety section on Google Play.

  • Export — you can export your data as JSON text from the Profile screen, through your phone's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Notifications — reminders and milestones are scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved.
  • Backups — your phone's own backup may include Stub's data, like any app's: iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android. That is Apple's or Google's system, governed by your phone's settings and their privacy policies. Stub's developer cannot see those backups.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your phone, deletion is entirely in your hands: the reset option in Stub's Profile screen erases everything the app holds, and so does deleting the app itself. We hold no copy, so there is nothing further to request from us. If your phone's backup is on, a copy may remain in your own iCloud or Google account until that backup is replaced or you delete it there.

If this ever changes

If a future version of Stub ever sends anything off your device — for example, anonymous crash reporting — this policy, the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated first, and any such feature will be described here before it ships.

A note on health information

Stub is intended for adult smokers (18 and over) who want to cut down. It is not for children and does not promote smoking. The health timeline, nicotine figures, and money estimates it shows are general information, not medical guidance. For advice about quitting smoking, talk to a doctor or a licensed cessation service.
